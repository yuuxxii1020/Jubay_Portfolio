About Me

Passionate About Capturing Authentic Moments

I’m Francis John Jubay, a photographer based in the Philippines, driven by a passion for capturing stories through the smallest details and the rawness of everyday life.

From the intricate beauty of macro subjects to the candid rhythm of street scenes, I focus on moments that often go unnoticed. My work explores aesthetic compositions and the natural world—where light, texture, and emotion come together to create something timeless.

Through macro, street, aesthetic, and nature photography, I aim to transform ordinary moments into meaningful visual stories—each frame crafted with intention, depth, and a sense of wonder.
